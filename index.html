What I Do

Services

Fractional Head of BD / Sales

Senior sales leadership for companies that need commercial direction but are not ready for a full-time hire. I help define sales strategy, improve pipeline discipline, coach teams, manage key opportunities and create a more structured approach to growth.

Market Entry & CEE Expansion

Support for international companies entering or expanding across Czechia, Slovakia and Central Europe. I help identify target segments, shape the commercial proposition, build senior relationships and create a practical route to market.

Enterprise Pipeline Build

For businesses that need to move beyond opportunistic sales and create a repeatable pipeline. I support ICP definition, prospect targeting, outreach strategy, CRM discipline, stakeholder mapping and conversion of high-value opportunities.

Helping companies deepen relationships with existing clients, unlock cross-sell opportunities and build referral-led growth. This includes partner mapping, account planning, commercial negotiation and senior stakeholder engagement.

Sales Team Coaching & Commercial Discipline

Practical support for sales teams that need clearer targets, better qualification, stronger messaging and more consistent execution. I bring structure, accountability and coaching based on real-world enterprise sales experience.
