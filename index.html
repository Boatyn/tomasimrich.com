How I work

Understand, prioritise, execute, build

Click a step.

Understand

We start with the commercial objective. What are you trying to achieve? Where is the opportunity? What's currently preventing you from getting there?

I look at your proposition, customers, pipeline, market and existing sales approach before recommending where to focus.

Prioritise

We identify the highest-value commercial opportunities and define a practical plan.

That might mean targeting a particular customer segment, entering a new market, developing a strategic partnership or progressing a small number of high-value enterprise opportunities.

Execute

This is where my approach differs from traditional consulting.

I can work directly on the commercial activity: approaching prospects, opening conversations, developing opportunities, working with stakeholders and helping progress deals.

Build

The goal isn't to create permanent dependence on an external consultant.

Where appropriate, I help establish the processes, commercial discipline and relationships that your internal team can continue to develop.
