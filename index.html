How engagements work

  • Flexible by structure Engagements run as fixed-term contracts or rolling arrangements, not permanent employment. Senior BD resource when and how you need it.
  • Defined scope, clear deliverables We agree what success looks like before we start. Pipeline targets, market entry milestones and partnership goals are all set out upfront.
  • Embedded, not advisory I work inside your business alongside your team. The output is real pipeline and real relationships, not a strategy document.
  • Selective by design I work with a small number of clients at any one time. Every engagement gets focused attention, not a stretched generalist.
  • Straightforward to engage A conversation, a brief, a contract. No lengthy procurement process. Operational within days.
